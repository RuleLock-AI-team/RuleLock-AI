What the system blocks

  • Coupon stacking and discount abuse that bypasses normal purchase checks.
  • Price and quantity manipulation across valid-looking transactions.
  • Fake COD orders and high-refusal behavior that consume delivery capacity.
  • Abuse spread across sessions and accounts designed to stay under single thresholds.
